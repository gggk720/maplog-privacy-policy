MapLog - Account & Data Deletion

MapLog by KimJaeSeong allows you to request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In-App Deletion

Step 1: Open the MapLog app on your device.
Step 2: Go to Settings (gear icon).
Step 3: Tap "Delete Account" or "Account Management".
Step 4: Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Option 2: Request via Email

Send an email to gggk720@naver.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please include:

  • The email address associated with your MapLog account
  • Your login method (Google, Kakao, or Apple)

We will process your request within 7 business days.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your travel logs, map pins, and uploaded photos will be permanently removed. Please back up any data you wish to keep before requesting deletion.
